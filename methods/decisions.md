- **2026-11-19:** Pilot. Ten government and club documents (Rwanda and DR Congo; English, French, and Spanish) were coded with codebook v0.2. The pilot found seven problems: passing documents lost relevant content; visits tied to the partnership were under-coded on centrality; contact with political leaders was not recorded; there was no code for a document that corrects press reports; scale had no rules for slogans, rankings, or continental labels; criticism that a text rebuts was undefined; and several smaller gaps (no business voice, no not-applicable value for outcomes, a single-value club response variable, no threshold for themes).
- **2026-11-19:** Codebook v0.3 issued to address them. Main changes: a wider `central` rule for visits and events; a fuller code set for passing documents; themes coded 0/1/2; scale separated from five Africa-reference variables; new `scrutiny_stance` and `political_contact`; new `correction_of_record` and `correction_of_reports`; three yes/no club response variables.
- **2026-11-19:** `political_contact` counts heads of state or government, ministers, and senior officials (agency and tourism board heads, ambassadors). Two conditions apply: the contact must be reported as an event, and representatives of both the club and the state must be present.

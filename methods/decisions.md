- Government annual reports are a **different genre** from individual government announcements. Their relevant sections and pages require separate coding rules; avoid mixing unweighted reports into press-release counts.
- The campaign website, football federation, and state news agency are not interchangeable with ministry-issued government communications.
- DRC 2025-10-03 page date corrected from earlier guessed October 1, using the ministry's actual publication line.
- Rwanda's 2023 French PSG press release is a possible translated duplicate of the English document and should not be counted twice without explicit translation-version coding.
- Comparisons require matched 2024–2026 time windows or event-based sampling, with 2018–2023 Rwanda material used for within-case trajectory.
- Claimed reach, engagement, survey effects and tourism outcomes must be attributed to the issuing institution and, where feasible, independently checked.
- Source inspection does not establish independent verification of substantive claims, complete screening, or intercoder reliability.
- Copyrighted third-party full-text stories and media assets are excluded from public redistribution.

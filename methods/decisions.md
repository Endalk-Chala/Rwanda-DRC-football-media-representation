- Government annual reports are a **different genre** from individual government announcements. Their relevant sections and pages require separate coding rules; avoid mixing unweighted reports into press-release counts.

- DRC 2025-10-03 page date corrected from earlier guessed October 1, using the ministry's actual publication line.

- Claimed reach, engagement, survey effects and tourism outcomes must be attributed to the issuing institution and, where feasible, independently checked.
- Source inspection does not establish independent verification of substantive claims, complete screening, or intercoder reliability.
- Copyrighted third-party full-text stories and media assets are excluded from public redistribution.

- **2026-11-19:** Pilot. Ten government and club documents (Rwanda and DR Congo; English, French, and Spanish) were coded with codebook v0.2. The pilot found seven problems: passing documents lost relevant content; visits tied to the partnership were under-coded on centrality; contact with political leaders was not recorded; there was no code for a document that corrects press reports; scale had no rules for slogans, rankings, or continental labels; criticism that a text rebuts was undefined; and several smaller gaps (no business voice, no not-applicable value for outcomes, a single-value club response variable, no threshold for themes).
- **2026-11-19:** Codebook v0.3 issued to address them. Main changes: a wider `central` rule for visits and events; a fuller code set for passing documents; themes coded 0/1/2; scale separated from five Africa-reference variables; new `scrutiny_stance` and `political_contact`; new `correction_of_record` and `correction_of_reports`; three yes/no club response variables.
- **2026-11-19:** `political_contact` counts heads of state or government, ministers, and senior officials (agency and tourism board heads, ambassadors). Two conditions apply: the contact must be reported as an event, and representatives of both the club and the state must be present.
- **2026-11-19:** The pilot codes are provisional proposals awaiting the researcher's review. Five of the ten rest on a machine translation or an extract and must be recoded from the original text. The press variables were not tested.
- **2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20:** All 58 government and club documents (38 government, 20 club) were coded. For each document an AI assistant read the text and proposed codes with a paraphrased evidence note; the researcher reviewed every document and accepted or changed the codes. This is single-coder work with AI-proposed codes. It is not independent double coding, and no reliability figure can be drawn from it. The codes are in `data/coding_pilot_v0.3.1.csv` (10 documents) and `data/coding_government_club_v0.3.1.csv` (48 documents); the `coder`, `researcher_review`, and `researcher_changes` columns record who did what.

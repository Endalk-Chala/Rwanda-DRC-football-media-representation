## Eligibility
Include official government statements covering launch, renewal, activation, impact claims, defense, implementation, or oversight. Exclude irrelevant sponsorships, duplicate syndicated pages, and non-government journalism. Keep excluded candidates and documented reasons.

2. Keep timestamped search logs, query strings, results counts, and all candidates.
3. Open each page and verify exact title, publication date, issuing body, language and substantive relevance.
4. Preserve source links and legally permitted archive links; deduplicate exact and near duplicates.
5. Preserve original French titles and document any translation procedures.
6. Analyze a shared 2025–2026 period for principal cross-country comparison; earlier Rwanda material is historical.

## No sampling claims
The initial register is a discovery inventory, not a representative sample. Neither case prevalence nor relative effectiveness can be estimated from it.

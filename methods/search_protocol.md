# Stage 1 Search and Screening Protocol (v0.2, 2026-11-13)

## Population and unit of analysis
**Target population:** All publicly findable, government-issued, substantively relevant texts about Rwanda's or DRC's sponsorship/partnership with European football clubs, available by **2026-11-13**, within the selected research window. This is an **aspirational population definition**, not a claim of complete enumeration.

**Unit:** One original dated publication, including press release, ministry update, government speech, council proceeding, or annual report. Separate government-authored texts from campaign organizations, football federations, European clubs, and news agencies. Identify exact relevant report sections and pages.

## Main comparison and historical context
**Main common comparison:** January 1, 2025 – November 13, 2026. Include both states using identical eligibility rules and search procedures. **Historical/contextual materials:** Rwanda 2018–2024; DRC 2024. Do not compute counts across unequal periods as comparative evidence.

## Search coverage
Official Rwanda domains: `rdb.rw`, relevant `gov.rw` ministries, diplomatic missions, Visit Rwanda accounts.
Official DRC domains: `sports.gouv.cd`, `communication.gouv.cd`, presidency, tourism ministry, cabinet archives. Campaign-domain and federation texts remain separate pending institutional classification.

Search across English and French. Document exact queries and run dates. Terms include:
- Rwanda: `"Visit Rwanda" Arsenal`, `"Visit Rwanda" PSG`, `"Visit Rwanda" Bayern`, `"Visit Rwanda" Atlético`, `"Visit Rwanda" partenariat football`
- DRC: `"RDC" "AS Monaco" partenariat`, `"R.D. Congo" "Cœur de l'Afrique" Barcelone`, `"RDC" "AC Milan" tourisme`, `"Congo" sponsor football club`, `"RDC" "Monaco" formation`
- Include synonyms, alternate spellings, French diacritics and domain-restricted/site-native archive searches.
- Search publication indexes by year and institution, not only modern search-engine results.

## Inclusion
Government-issued and publicly accessible document whose content substantively addresses the named European club partnership, its planned aims, implementation, assessments, parliamentary scrutiny or official response to criticism. Retain out-of-scope hits in an exclusion log.

## Exclusion
Duplicates, source-only image galleries, unrelated sports, generic tourism pages without a relevant club link, external journalism, unauthenticated campaign websites, and texts outside the final date window for the main comparative sample. Do not delete discovery records; mark decisions and reasons.

## Verification and audit
For each candidate: confirm title, institution, original date, original language, live URL, document type, access date and relevant passages; register retrieval errors, archive snapshots, duplicate relationships, and inclusion rationale. Attribute each outcome statistic to its speaker/source and check its denominator and measurement method. Record original-language evidence and translation decisions.

**Current status:** 33 dated government candidates + 9 separately labeled supplementary references. Search not exhaustive; no complete denominator, finalized sample, or inter-coder reliability established.

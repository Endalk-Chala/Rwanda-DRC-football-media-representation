# Provisional Codebook — Not Yet Coded

**Unit:** a unique official government webpage. The 23 candidates have not undergone systematic eligibility screening or full-text coding.

## Proposed variables
- `tourism_promotion` (0/1/unclear): tourism explicitly promoted.
- `investment_development` (0/1/unclear): investment or economic development presented.
- `sport_youth_development` (0/1/unclear): training or sport capacity emphasized.
- `cultural_identity` (0/1/unclear): national culture or heritage explicitly invoked.
- `international_visibility` (0/1/unclear): global reach, reputation or exposure explicitly invoked.
- `conflict_accountability` (0/1/unclear): regional conflict, sponsorship criticism, public spending, or oversight addressed.
- `claimed_outcome` (0/1): government asserts achieved impact.
- `independent_evidence_in_document` (yes/no/unclear): separately sourced evidence with disclosed methods rather than self-report.
- `primary_narrative`: tourism / development / recognition / sport capacity / political defense / mixed / other.

## Coding protocol
Independently pilot approximately 10 items using French- and English-competent coders. Document ambiguous cases, revise rules before full coding, and retain independent coding alongside adjudication. Compute intercoder agreement only after completed double coding. Do not interpret source titles or discovery notes as analysis.
